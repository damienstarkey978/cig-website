Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who does Custom Intelligence Group serve?

Custom Intelligence Group serves businesses that need practical help with repetitive workflows, disconnected systems, or operational visibility. CIG works especially well with insurance, construction, logistics, legal, accounting, and other service teams.

Where does CIG work?

CIG is based in Jacksonville, Florida, and serves Jacksonville, Ponte Vedra, St. Augustine, and nearby Northeast Florida communities. Work can be handled in person or remotely as appropriate for the project.

What does CIG build?

CIG builds custom business automations, AI employees and agents, software integrations, document and email workflows, internal knowledge assistants, and operational dashboards.

Does CIG replace employees?

No. CIG designs AI to support people by handling repetitive sorting, summarizing, routing, reminding, and reporting work. The operating principle is that AI does not replace people; it replaces burnout work.

How does a project begin?

A project begins with an onboarding intake at customintelligencegroup.com/#onboarding. CIG uses the information about the workflow, systems, and desired outcome to determine a sensible next step.

Can CIG connect to our existing software?

CIG can evaluate and connect existing tools such as forms, email, CRM, spreadsheets, accounting platforms, and other business systems when the available access and workflow make that practical.

Does AI make business or professional decisions for us?

CIG can design reviewable workflows, but responsible people remain in control of business, legal, insurance, accounting, safety, and other professional decisions. Systems should route exceptions and require approval where appropriate.

How much does custom AI work cost?

CIG does not publish invented one-size-fits-all prices because scope depends on the workflow, systems, data, controls, and level of implementation required. The onboarding intake is the starting point for discussing fit and scope.

How long does implementation take?

Implementation timing depends on the workflow, integrations, access, testing, and review requirements. CIG determines a realistic sequence after understanding the specific project rather than promising a universal timeline.

How can a business contact CIG?

A business can start at customintelligencegroup.com/#onboarding or call 904-664-7032. CIG is located at 6005 Powers Ave #210, Jacksonville, FL 32217.
